# Inclusive mixed casting

The collection now has 61 reviewed trope scenes with people of color in prominent roles out of 181 playable trope scenes (33.7%). This comprises 57 new original raster edits and four retained inclusive scenes. Mixed romantic couples, families and friend groups appear throughout; casting includes Black, East Asian, South Asian, Latino, Indigenous American, Arab American and white fictional characters. These are artistic casting directions, not ethnicity predictions about real people.

Distribution: Core tropes: 12; Christmas: 10; Fall: 8; Spring: 8; Summer: 9; No Season room scenes: 14. Representation is prominent in the scene, rather than counted solely through background crowds. Some scenes are solo or family/friend scenes and are preserved as such.

All 57 new sources were individually inspected for faces, hands, limbs, expressions, physical interactions, scene identity, illustration quality and casting. All 61 selected scenes have paired before/after 384px and 64px contact sheets; full-inventory before/after sheets cover all 213 original files. Unchanged scene sources, standard exports and detail exports were verified by hashes. The 57 edits have both 384px standard and 768px detail WebP exports. This is an agent artistic review, not a blind viewer study.

The approved all-season CHEESE LOUISE POD emblem, other 124 active scene sources and 31 archived/legacy files remain unchanged. Canonical IDs, labels, 70/30 mobile layout, deterministic dealing, scoring, sound, animations, multiplayer, private HQ data and paused Android source were not modified. The service-worker cache version is advanced to refresh the replacement art. No racial categories or representation quotas were added to game logic; 33.7% describes the illustration library, not a guaranteed fraction on each randomized board.

Built-in image_gen performed the artwork edits. No global skin recolors, SVG substitutes or programmatic creative edits were used. A connection-interrupted batch required five scene retries; all 57 final sources are saved and reviewed. Actual prompts, casting directions, saved PNG paths and production hashes are recorded in inclusive-cast-prompts.json.

Validation: 290 unit tests, complete standard/detail asset validation, five-size inventory QA at both standard and high density, selected/winning/all-marked states, and existing audio/replay/offline/multiplayer/design/season regressions. Evidence: artifacts/inclusive-cast/index.html. Deployment and public hash verification are reported separately for the tested commit.
